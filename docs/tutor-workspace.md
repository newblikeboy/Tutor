# Tutor teaching workspace

The tutor update follows the parent booking direction. Parent payment confirmation activates regular tuition without tutor acceptance. Trial requests and consented replacement invitations still have their own acceptance steps.

## Teaching flow

1. Complete the application and staff approval for the teaching scope and fees.
2. Set teaching windows and planned leave in Teaching hours. Fees show all staff-confirmed offerings, including six-class weekly and twenty-four-class monthly Home Tuition packages. These counts do not impose calendar deadlines.
3. Home combines regular and trial lessons, the next lesson, active learner count, trial requests, confirmed-booking alerts and follow-up tasks. The teaching-date filter uses India Standard Time and remains in the URL. Paused arrangements cannot be recorded or joined.
4. My learners contains an assigned learner's saved class, board, teaching language, booked subjects, mode, duration and links to packages. Current approved Home assignments and confirmed Home trials may show the guardian's saved home address; completed arrangements do not expose the current address.
5. The learner report has Overview, Subjects, Class history and Feedback, with URL-backed date/subject/tutor filters. An active or paused assignment is required to open a learner report. Tutors see their own lessons and trials under their assigned packages, including their completed-package history while that learner remains assigned, and the saved plans for those packages. After the last assignment finishes, use Package history to read or correct saved lesson records; the current learner profile is no longer returned. They cannot open the parent-only cross-tutor report, edit a learner profile, or retrieve a former assignment after handover.
6. Accept a requested trial, teach it, then submit notes, next practice and feedback. Completed trials say feedback submitted and retain the tutor's own submitted record. Cancelled, declined and historical reviewed trials remain in trial history. No new mentor feedback or approval step is added.
7. Confirmed regular bookings appear automatically after provider-verified payment. Home shows an unread activity alert for the assigned tutor. Booking cards and immutable agreements show all booked subjects, teaching mode, duration and saved prices. Unpaid or expired selections, including cancelled unpaid checkouts, do not become tutor booking cards.
8. Use Plan subject on a future scheduled lesson to assign one booked subject. Classes 1–5 can record individual subject detail inside All Subjects; upper classes can only choose booked subjects covered by the current approval. Planning does not change class count, saved prices, attendance or finance.
9. Present-attendance lesson recording requires bounded subject/topic observations, homework status, parent feedback and next practice. Tests and marks are optional, and missing results are never inferred. Absent and disputed attendance retain their separate actual evidence/resolution controls.
10. Progress can be corrected for seven days after the server-stamped lesson recording, including after the final class completes a package. Historical lessons without that timestamp use their saved end time. Trial feedback uses the same seven-day window after completion. Corrections are versioned and audited; progress corrections do not reopen a package or change attendance, notes, homework text or money. Older records remain readable. Home's missing-progress tasks only include records inside this correction window; overdue scheduled lessons and trial feedback remain actionable.
11. Current bookings and Package history are separate regular-class views. Completed/cancelled archives contain class evidence, plans and saved agreements. Tutor archive access does not reopen files, conversations, handovers or scheduling controls. Reads include histories longer than the former 100-record cap.

## Online lessons

An assigned approved tutor can prepare a Zoom meeting for a scheduled online regular lesson or confirmed online trial. A durable outbox job creates and verifies the provider meeting. The tutor's account email becomes the alternative host, with provider email notifications disabled. The tutor signs into Zoom using that email; no shared host credentials or reusable host start URL are sent to the browser.

Parent and tutor join controls open fifteen minutes before the lesson. The server rechecks current ownership/assignment, active booking, approved scope, actual lesson status, timing and the provider-verified URL before issuing the participant link with `Cache-Control: no-store`. Rescheduling, cancellation, development completion and handover remove the old meeting association and queue provider cleanup. A create that finishes after its association changes queues orphan cleanup; cancelled jobs that have never attempted creation skip the provider POST.

Retries reconcile uncertain creation by its unique opaque topic and durable provider ID. Failed jobs expose retry controls and never fabricate a meeting. The configured Zoom host is a single resource: overlapping prepared lessons/interviews are rejected, including retained interview bookings from before this update. Multiple independent host accounts and automatic paid license provisioning are outside this configured adapter.

The operator must configure Zoom credentials and ensure each teaching tutor is an eligible licensed alternative host in the Zoom account. These are Zoom requirements, not automatic application signup benefits. See [Zoom alternative-host requirements](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0082130) and [Zoom meeting API settings](https://godevelopers.zoom.us/docs/api/meetings/). No paid provisioning, real meetings or real payment capture are performed by the acceptance tests.
