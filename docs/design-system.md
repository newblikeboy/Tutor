# Design foundation

Warm academic editorial direction. Provisional text wordmark: Tutor Platform. No invented founder, statistics or endorsements. Label illustrative imagery; never present generated people as actual tutors or learners.

Tokens: navy #13213C, teal #087F8C (large elements), darker teal #076B76 for normal text/buttons; paper #F8F7F4; white surfaces; text #152238; secondary #546172; border #E2E6EB. Amber is a supporting surface, with dark text. Status always includes words/icons.

Self-hosted OFL Manrope Latin + Noto Sans Devanagari; body 16px/1.65, large editorial headings, Hindi natural line-height and less aggressive tracking. 8px spacing with 4px subdivisions; 12/16px radii; subtle shadows; 180ms interaction transitions; reduced-motion override. Public width 1248px.

Homepage (updated 2026-09-23): an equal-column editorial/photo hero at 1024px and above, stacked below; consistent 1248px section edges and 20px mobile gutters. It follows the approved account palette: paper #F5F3ED, deep ink #15353B, sage #DFE8BC and readable green #57744C. `styles/home.css` scopes these tokens to the landing shell, preserving account and workspace rendering. Three evenly spaced support pillars and numbered process cards lead to actual Go-backed tutor listings. A dark continuity section pairs explanatory text with a clearly illustrative family learning record; a native details/summary FAQ and centred sage call to action finish the page. The generated photograph, provenance and responsive asset choices are documented in assets.md. Search: filter rail/drawer, data-backed cards. Profile: scoped evidence and sticky request summary. Parent: learner focus and next action. Tutor: assigned work and lesson tasks. Mentor/admin: contextual queues and reasoned decisions, no decorative metrics.

44px target product standard, visible focus, skip link, semantic headings, labelled fields, native date/time fields, Radix dialog focus trap/restore, live announcements. 360/390/768/1024/1440 responsive targets. No localStorage except language.

`/components` demonstrates the implemented component states in both languages. Browser artifacts and inspected status are recorded in progress.md; an image existing alone is not acceptance.

Private workspace (2026-09-23): `/workspace`, `/match` and `/apply` use a distinct application shell, with a 236px ink sidebar, compact utility bar, restrained headings and paper work surfaces. At 1000px and below the rail becomes a keyboard-operable modal drawer. `styles/workspace.css` scopes the shared account colours to this shell. The public header/footer are replaced by role navigation and a quiet private-workspace footer. Login/signup and the public landing retain their own layouts.

Parents have a deliberate first-use invitation, learner switcher, current focus, sessions, saved needs and reviewed progress. Tutors have actual session counts, teaching tasks and application stages reflecting saved status. Mentors have assigned assessment/review views; admins have tutor scopes and decision history. Search/status filters apply only to records returned by the authorised API, with explicit no-match states; counts describe the current returned records rather than global network totals. URL state preserves selected learners, views and filters across reloads. Loading, session failure, retry, sign-out, access denial and real empty records remain explicit. No placeholder notifications, earnings, invented charts or fake records are added.

Windows Chromium review includes English/Hindi desktop/mobile, five responsive widths, keyboard skip and drawer focus, default axe rules, CSS 200% reflow, and the real application/approval/trial/progress forms. Two individually inspected first-use parent baselines protect the private shell without depending on changing audit dates. Other role screenshots are review evidence, not deterministic pixel comparisons.

Account redesign (user request, 2026-09-23): `/login`, `/signup`, and staff sign-in use a dedicated editorial shell in `styles/auth.css`. Paper #F5F3ED, deep ink #15353B, muted sage #DFE8BC; readable 16px form text and 14px labels. The desktop spread pairs a custom fraction/notebook illustration with a focused form. Mobile puts the form first and reduces the illustration to a short supporting panel. Shared patterns include account-role radios, labelled password reveal, inline validation, pending submit, honest recovery help, and preserved return intent. Parent/tutor signup shares the design; staff use assigned email credentials with no public staff registration. No new raster assets, fabricated statistics, testimonials or third-party brand assets were introduced. Four account baselines were opened before promotion.
