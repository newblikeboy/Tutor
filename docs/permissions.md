# Implemented access boundary

Google interview additions (2026-10-11): only active mentors can connect/disconnect their own Google authorization. Only the assigned mentor schedules, reconciles and assesses an applicant or approves/rejects scope. The callback binds to the initiating password session and one-time state. Tutors can read/join only their own interview; assigned mentors can join; admin/support/finance/parents cannot use the interview join redirect. Admins retain token-free read-only connection/synchronization and assessment visibility. See [Google interviews](google-interviews.md).

Only the assigned mentor can approve or reject an application. Rejection records applicant-visible feedback and cancels any scheduled interview; admin academic decision writes are denied. Admins can separately reopen rejected applications for mentor review.

| Actor | Permitted in development/test | Denied |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous | Read approved public tutor DTOs; email/password signup as an adult parent/tutor; sign in | Staff registration, academic approval, private learner/application/lesson records |
| Parent / adult learner | Own learners/consents/requirements/trials; agreements/classes/plans/handover consent; current conversation/files; own billing/refund requests; own service/privacy/safeguarding requests | Other households; self-granted staff roles; academic approval; provider/refund approval |
| Tutor | Own application/documents/availability; scoped trial and agreement acceptance; assigned classes/lesson evidence/conversation/files; replacement acceptance | Self-approval; all-learner browsing; unrelated tuition; access after handover or enrollment closure; finance operations |
| Mentor | Read assigned applications; arrange/retry Google Meet interviews, finalize fees, approve requested subject/class/teaching-mode scope or reject pending applications with feedback; assigned tuition learning plans, attendance dispute resolution and handover preparation | Self-approval; changes to another mentor's records; suspension/termination; unrelated family learning records; finance and other people's safeguarding data |
| Admin | Application queues/details/files/history; route pending applications to active mentors; reasoned suspension/restoration of existing unexpired scope/termination/reopening and follow-up outcomes; eligible service/safeguarding decisions; finance reconciliation and independent refund review | Interview or fee changes; application approval/rejection or scope updates; unrelated family learning data/files; unclaimed case contents; approving own refund request |
| Support | Own requests plus eligible non-safeguarding queue; claim then respond/wait/resolve | Academic dashboards/learning files; finance operations; another person's safeguarding data; other operators' case contents |
| Finance | Payment/refund/journal records, payment jobs and reconciliation; independent refund review; own service requests | Academic dashboards/learning files; another person's service/safeguarding contents; self-approved refunds |

Production authentication follows the explicit password configuration in `production-auth.md` and mandatory public signup verification in `email.md`. Staff login accepts provisioned email/password credentials; there is no staff role selector or public staff signup. A role/version update invalidates earlier sessions. Login rotation/logout revoke prior sessions and clear private TanStack Query state. Expiry is checked independently of TTL cleanup. Guardian and tutor approval gates remain separate from sign-in.

All authenticated adults can update their own name/language, change a password after current-password verification and revoke their own other sessions. Notification access is owner-only. The CLI creates staff accounts under an explicit operator identity/reason and never changes an existing public account's role. It does not implement invitations, staff MFA or a permission-management UI.

Mentor approval follows requested subject/class/mode boundaries, including Home/Online and the Classes 1–5 All Subjects limit. Specialist authorization, granular invites, privacy execution and safeguarding protective-action/escalation workflows remain open. A submitted case is an actual persisted request, not proof of legal compliance or completed deletion. Tutor metadata for ended trials is retained with learner name/notes/review redacted; operator retention review is required. The `/workspace` page sends finance/support to their functional work areas (`/billing`, `/cases`); the academic dashboard API continues to deny these roles.

The October 11 tutor-approval override supersedes the earlier administrator assessment permission: only the assigned active mentor handles Google interviews, fees and scope approval. Initial approval requires a confirmed Google interview with a manually submitted approval recommendation, complete saved fees and requested subject/class/mode bounds. The requested four-rating Google interview assessment supersedes the earlier scorecard prohibition; no conflict declaration, legacy six-score evidence, separate employment clearance, internal-note writer, approval reason or extra mentor selection is required. Historical evidence remains private and retained. This does not grant public role promotion. Suspended/terminated tutors lose active sessions and teaching-route access, but can sign back in for their own decision, account and support. Termination has no reinstatement route. See [staff operations](staff-operations.md).

Only an assigned approved tutor can prepare Google Meet for their online lessons. Parents and tutors join after current ownership/assignment/scope/timing checks. Own Google connection and cancellation recovery are available through Account; a paused tutor may renew existing authorization to resolve hosted work, without regaining teaching access. Unapproved applicants cannot authorize lesson hosting. See [Google lessons](google-lessons.md).
