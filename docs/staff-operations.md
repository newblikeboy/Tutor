# Staff operations

Implemented 2026-09-23 at `/workspace` after provisioned staff sign-in. The user requested working application, Zoom interview, approval, suspension and termination tools in the staff account. Changes were made directly without the prohibited update-design skill. Executed verification is recorded in progress.md.

Admin and assigned academic reviewers have application queues, interview scheduling, evidence-based scorecards and scoped approval. Admins can assign reviewers, suspend/reinstate, terminate and record follow-up outcomes. Support and finance retain their separate roles and cannot access these academic tools. Administrators who assess a tutor choose an ongoing academic mentor before approval, keeping teaching continuity separate from recruitment review.

The staff navigation now groups Interviews inside Applications (Assessments for mentors) and Follow-ups inside Tutor network. Overview, decision history, payment records, updates, support and account security remain available because they serve distinct operational tasks. Existing interview/follow-up URLs still work. Overview shows one queue at a time; applicant records use Application, Review & decision, Documents and History tabs. Staff payment records and payment follow-ups also have separate tabs. Selected tabs live in the URL and support reload and browser Back.

Inactive lists unmount and do not start their data requests. Applicant review forms mount on first use and keep unfinished inputs while switching tabs; their member queries pause while hidden. Documents and audit history load on demand. Tab arrows/Home/End move focus, and Enter/Space select a tab. Tabs wrap on mobile and use the existing bilingual paper/ink/sage design. These changes reduce initial data loading, not database query execution time; no timing or throughput improvement is claimed without measurement.

Staff choose a date and duration; the Go worker creates the interview through Zoom Server-to-Server OAuth and records the confirmed join link. Rescheduling updates the same meeting, and cancellation queues deletion. The applicant sees the confirmed appointment and can download a calendar event. Pending/failed confirmation is visible and prevents assessment; assigned staff can retry failed operations. Email invitations are not implemented. Stored instants are UTC with an explicit IANA timezone; the current form labels India time. See [Cloudinary/Zoom setup and verification limits](recruitment-integrations.md).

Decisions use the current application version. Reviewers confirm no conflict of interest; self-assessment is rejected. Scheduling contends on reviewer/applicant day guards across API instances. Rescheduling or reassignment releases old reservations transactionally. A future interview cannot be marked assessed or absent. Assessment requires six scores and evidence. Approval records its scope and expiry; it is never implied by signup. Internal notes and immutable decision/evidence history are staff-only.

Suspension and termination revoke existing sessions, stop new bookings and remove tutor access to teaching/learner routes. The tutor can sign back in to see the decision and access their account/application/support. Termination has no reinstatement path. Existing trials, learning records and financial records are retained. A durable operator follow-up opens or reopens, displaying actual affected trial/tuition counts. Resolving that review records the operator's outcome; it does not fabricate a cancellation, transfer or refund. An expired suspended approval requires reassessment.

The new core workflow only uses existing `applications`, `audit`, `guards`, `outbox`, `users`, and `sessions` collections. `go run ./cmd/migrate -staff-only` adds the staff indexes and optional interview validation to existing collections without creating or deleting collections. The full migration includes this step. This does not resolve Atlas capacity or install earlier pending tuition/file/billing extension collections.

The additive staff migration is installed on the configured development Atlas database. Its earlier readiness, provisioned sample-admin login, six staff read endpoints and logout checks passed on ports 5173/8080. All 15 existing collections remain; no collections or retained databases were deleted. The wider tuition/files/billing migration still needs the existing Atlas capacity blocker resolved. Automatic Zoom creation is implemented in the 2026-09-24 checkpoint; external invitations, granular staff administration/MFA, specialist assessments, CMS and broader reporting remain separate work. No public deployment or external invitation occurred.
