The current approval workflow follows the operator's 2026-10-11 simplification: three sections, **Zoom interview**, **Fees**, and **Subjects & teaching modes**. Submission automatically assigns an active mentor. Only that mentor schedules or retries Zoom, sets fees and approves requested subjects, classes and modes. Approval records the same mentor for ongoing academic assignment; no second mentor selector is required. Admins see these sections read-only and can route pending applications to active mentors. Admin account access operations (suspension, restoration of an existing unexpired scope, termination and reopening) remain separate. Support and finance cannot access these academic tools.

The staff navigation now groups Interviews inside Applications (Tutor applications for mentors) and Follow-ups inside Tutor network. Overview, decision history, payment records, updates, support and account security remain available because they serve distinct operational tasks. Existing interview/follow-up URLs still work, including the mentor `view=assessments` route. Overview shows one queue at a time; applicant records use Application, Review & decision, Documents and History tabs. Staff payment records and payment follow-ups also have separate tabs. Selected tabs live in the URL and support reload and browser Back.

The mentor dashboard redesign (2026-10-11) prioritises **New applications** by default, with a **Review applications** action, actual authorised counts and the next scheduled interview with its provider-confirmed join controls. The applications queue offers search, status and teaching-mode filters; mentors already see only their own assignments, so no redundant reviewer filter is shown. Displayed loaded counts come from the received pages, not an invented total. The three-step guide introduces the existing approval workflow. Desktop has a light sidebar, concise application columns and a two-column Interview/fees layout followed by teaching scope; mobile stacks these views and preserves drawer keyboard/focus behaviour. Sign-in and other role designs are unchanged.

Inactive lists unmount and do not start their data requests. Applicant review forms mount on first use and keep unfinished inputs while switching tabs. Documents and audit history load on demand. Tab arrows/Home/End move focus, and Enter/Space select a tab. Tabs wrap on mobile and use the existing English-only paper/ink/sage design. These changes reduce initial data loading, not database query execution time; no timing or throughput improvement is claimed without measurement.

The assigned mentor chooses a date and duration; the Go worker creates the interview through the mentor's authorized Google Calendar and records the confirmed join link. Rescheduling updates the same meeting, and cancellation queues deletion. The applicant sees the confirmed appointment and can download a calendar event. Pending/failed confirmation is visible and prevents approval; only the assigned mentor can retry failed operations. Fees can be saved before the appointment or while provider confirmation is pending. Google Calendar sends participant event notices; SMTP delivery remains independently configured. Stored instants are UTC with an explicit IANA timezone; the current form labels India time. See [Cloudinary/Google setup and verification limits](recruitment-integrations.md).

Decisions use the current application version; self-approval is rejected. Scheduling contends on mentor/applicant/Google-calendar guards across API instances. Rescheduling or reassignment releases old reservations transactionally. Initial approval requires a provider-confirmed Google appointment with a manually submitted approval recommendation, complete saved fees for the approved modes, and a scope inside requested teaching areas. All Subjects remains limited to Classes 1–5. Approval completes the interview and releases interview guards transactionally, records its approval timestamp and six-month scope expiry, and publishes the tutor. Signup does not imply approval. Updating a current unexpired approval does not require a new interview; expired scopes must return to mentor review for a new interview.

The assigned mentor sees **Approve tutor** and **Reject application** together in the subject/mode section. Approval stays disabled until the interview assessment and fees are ready. Rejection requires a 10–1000 character reason visible to the applicant, but does not require an interview or saved fees. It is available only for pending submitted/review/scheduled/legacy assessed applications. It persists the existing `declined` status, preserves historical records, releases interview reservations and queues deletion of a scheduled Google meeting. A pending/failed provider operation must finish or be retried first. Admins cannot reject applications; they can reopen rejected applications for mentor review. Approved tutors use the separate administrative access controls.

Conflict confirmation, six-score assessment/evidence, separate employment eligibility clearance, internal-note writing, approval reasons, change-request forms and the additional academic mentor picker are removed from the current approval interface and decision input. Legacy statuses, scores, eligibility records and immutable audit snapshots remain readable and are not converted to fabricated results. Rejection, cancellation, rescheduling and administrative operations retain their operational reasons. No migration rewrites existing live applications or booked fee agreements. Historical non-Google interviews cannot grant a new approval or expose retired join controls; cancel the local booking and schedule Google Meet.

Suspension and termination revoke existing sessions, stop new bookings and remove tutor access to teaching/learner routes. The tutor can sign back in to see the decision and access their account/application/support. Termination has no reinstatement path. Existing trials, learning records and financial records are retained. A durable operator follow-up opens or reopens, displaying actual affected trial/tuition counts. Resolving that review records the operator's outcome; it does not fabricate a cancellation, transfer or refund. An expired suspended approval requires reassessment.

The new core workflow only uses existing `applications`, `audit`, `guards`, `outbox`, `users`, and `sessions` collections. `go run ./cmd/migrate -staff-only` adds the staff indexes and optional interview validation to existing collections without creating or deleting collections. The full migration includes this step. This does not resolve Atlas capacity or install earlier pending tuition/file/billing extension collections.
